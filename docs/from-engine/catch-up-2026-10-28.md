## What changed

Every route below takes `Authorization: Bearer <key>` and, on a write, `X-Idempotency-Key: <uuid>`, as every route does.

### 1. A holder has a kind ⚠️

- Every holder response carries `is_person`: `false` a bank holder, holding real accounts; `true` a person holder, holding people. The holder's activity-log snapshots carry it too.
- Every account is on a holder and has its holder's kind. `holder_id` is never `null` on any account response; a person made by `POST /people` read `null` before.
- **Making a person:** `POST /holders` takes an optional `is_person` (omitted: `false`; `null` or a non-boolean: `422`, `INVALID_FORMAT`). With `is_person: true`, every item of `accounts` is created a person. ALEJO in soles and in dollars is one write:

  ```
  POST /v1/holders
  {"id": "<uuid>", "name": "ALEJO", "is_person": true,
   "accounts": [{"id": "<uuid>", "name": "ALEJO", "currency_code": "PEN"},
                {"id": "<uuid>", "name": "ALEJO", "currency_code": "USD"}]}
  ```

  `POST /accounts` with `holder_id` naming a person holder adds a person to it, by every rule `POST /accounts` already applies. `PUT /holders/{id}` does not take `is_person` (`UNKNOWN_FIELD`).
- **Order:** a holder's `sort_order` is numbered per kind, as an account's is. `GET /holders` lists every bank holder first, then every person holder, each by `sort_order`, then creation time. `POST /holders` with no `sort_order` appends among the holders of its kind; `PUT /holders/{id}`'s `sort_order` places it among its own kind.
- **Names:** a holder's name is unique among your live holders of both kinds, ignoring case, as before.
- **Statement code:** a person holder may carry one like any holder. It is held against holders of both kinds (`STATEMENT_CODE_EXISTS`) and kept when the holder changes kind.

### 2. The kind changes on the holder, whole ⚠️

- **Added:** `POST /holders/{id}/make-person` and `POST /holders/{id}/make-account`, no body. They turn the holder and every account of it, deleted ones included, into the other kind in one write. The holder keeps its id, name, `statement_code`, `is_archived` and `account_ids` and is not deleted. Each account keeps its id, name, currency, colour, transactions, opening balance and balance. The holder joins the end of its new kind's holder list, and each live account the end of its new kind's account list, in the order the holder's accounts had. `200` with the holder. The holder and each account bump `version` and write an `UPDATED` entry.
- **Refused,** nothing written, the first that applies answering:

  | # | When | Status | Key | Reason |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | no live holder of yours has that id | `404` | | `NOT_FOUND` |
  | 2 | the holder is archived | `409` | `holder_id` | `ACCOUNT_ARCHIVED` |
  | 3 | it is already that kind | `409` | `holder_id` | `KIND_UNCHANGED` |
  | 4 | it has ever had a statement, a deleted one included | `409` | `holder_id` | `HAS_RECONCILIATION` |

  Plus `409` `CONCURRENT_CHANGE` on `holder_id` when an account is put on the holder while the conversion is being written, as on the holder's archive: send it again.
- **Removed:** `POST /accounts/{id}/make-person` and `POST /accounts/{id}/make-account` now answer `404`. Replacement: the holder routes above, on the account's holder. An account no longer needs to be alone on its holder, and the holder is no longer deleted. `HOLDER_HAS_OTHER_ACCOUNTS` is sent by no operation.

### 3. A person takes no statement and no bank file ⚠️

- **Added:** reason `PERSON_TAKES_NO_STATEMENT`, `422`, nothing written:
  - `POST /statements` naming a person holder, on `holder_id`, right after `INVALID_REFERENCE` and before the `balances` checks (a statement sent again still answers `ID_EXISTS` first);
  - `POST /statements/import` for a person, on `account_id`, right after `INVALID_REFERENCE` and before `NO_STATEMENT_CODE`, on a dry run as on an apply;
  - `POST /transactions/import` (the bank's Excel download) for a person, on `account_id`, right after `INVALID_REFERENCE`, on a dry run as on an apply.

  Both uploads judge the account once the file reads: a file refused whole still answers first.
- **Before → after:** a person made by `POST /people` took the bank download, and was refused `INVALID_REFERENCE` by the PDF upload; both are now `PERSON_TAKES_NO_STATEMENT`.
- `make-account`'s `HAS_RECONCILIATION` message is now `"Must be a holder that has never had a statement, live or deleted: a holder that has had a statement keeps its kind."`; `make-person`'s is unchanged.

### 4. Archiving is on the holder only ⚠️

- **Removed:** `POST /accounts/{id}/archive` and `POST /accounts/{id}/unarchive` now answer `404`. Replacement: `POST /holders/{id}/archive` and `/unarchive` on the account's holder, which archive every account of it, a person holder's people as a bank holder's accounts. `ARCHIVE_THE_HOLDER` is sent by no operation.
- `/dashboard`'s `people` and `archived_people` are unchanged: one row per account. Archiving ALEJO's holder moves both its rows to `archived_people` (`?include_archived=true`).

### 5. A person moves between person holders ⚠️

- `POST /accounts/{id}/move` now moves a person onto another person holder, exactly as a real account between bank holders: everything but `holder_id` stays, and the holder it leaves holding no live account is deleted in the same write. **Pairing ALEJO:** move ALEJO in dollars onto the holder of ALEJO in soles; the dollar account's old holder is deleted.
- **Added:** reason `HOLDER_KIND_MISMATCH`, `409` on `holder_id`: a move onto a holder of the other kind, a person onto a bank holder or a real account onto a person holder, nothing written.
- `PERSON_NOT_MOVED` is sent by no operation. The move's refusals, the first that applies answering: `ACCOUNT_ARCHIVED`, `STATEMENT_KEEPS_ACCOUNT`, `HOLDER_UNCHANGED`, then the target (`INVALID_REFERENCE`, `HOLDER_KIND_MISMATCH`, `HOLDER_CURRENCY_TAKEN`, `HOLDER_HAS_STATEMENT`).

### 6. `POST /people` is removed, and every existing person is on a holder ⚠️

- **Removed:** `POST /people` now answers `404`. Replacement: `POST /holders` with `is_person: true` and the person's accounts (section 1), or `POST /accounts` on a person holder. Both take the `id`, `name`, `currency_code`, `color` and `sort_order` `POST /people` took, judged by the same rules, and answer the same account shape.
- **The owner's people after the update:** every person that was on no holder got a person holder of its own: named after the person, no statement code, archived or deleted with it, appended to the person holders' list. Two live people sharing a name: the older keeps it, the later one's holder takes its currency ("ALEJO", "ALEJO USD"). Nothing pairs them; pair them with the move (section 5). These holders have no `CREATED` activity entry. On the owner's ledger the update found no person on no holder, so it created no holder and changed no person.

### 8. Unpairing is one write (`ec1b54dcf8b39917`) ⚠️

- **Added:** `POST /accounts/{id}/move` takes `holder` in place of `holder_id`: a new holder, the `POST /holders` body without `is_person` and `accounts` (required `id`, `name`; optional `statement_code`, `sort_order`, same rules and defaults). The holder is created of the account's kind and the account moved onto it in one write, all or nothing: a refused move creates no holder, and a refused holder moves nothing. The holder the account leaves is deleted when left empty, as before.

  ```
  POST /v1/accounts/<usd account id>/move
  {"holder": {"id": "<uuid>", "name": "ALEJO USD"}}
  ```

- Activity: the new holder's `CREATED` entry, the account's `UPDATED` entry, and the `DELETED` entry of a holder left empty.
- **Refused,** nothing written, the first that applies answering; every refusal of the new holder is keyed `holder.<field>`:

  | # | When | Status | Key | Reason |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | the request's shape (`holder.is_person`, `holder.accounts` are `UNKNOWN_FIELD`); a blank `holder.name` | `422` | `holder.<field>` | as named, `EMPTY` for the name |
  | 2 | `holder.id` taken, live or deleted | `409` | `holder.id` | `ID_EXISTS` |
  | 3 | no live account of yours has that id | `404` | | `NOT_FOUND` |
  | 4 | the holder cap | `422` `LIMIT_REACHED` | `holder.id` | `CAP_REACHED` |
  | 5 | the account is archived | `409` | `account_id` | `ACCOUNT_ARCHIVED` |
  | 6 | the account has ever been in a statement | `409` | `account_id` | `STATEMENT_KEEPS_ACCOUNT` |
  | 7 | `holder.name` held by another live holder, ignoring case | `409` | `holder.name` | `NAME_EXISTS` |
  | 8 | `holder.statement_code` held by another live holder | `409` | `holder.statement_code` | `STATEMENT_CODE_EXISTS` |

  A `holder.id` another user holds is found only when the holder is written, after every other refusal, and answers the same `409` `ID_EXISTS` on `holder.id`.

- ⚠️ **Exactly one of `holder_id` and `holder`**, a `null` counting as not sent. Neither: `422` on both keys, `REQUIRED` (before: `{}` answered `REQUIRED` on `holder_id` alone). Both: `422` on both keys, `CONFLICTING_PARAMETERS`. A move with `holder_id` is otherwise unchanged.
- Idempotency covers the whole write: the same key answers the stored response; the same body under a new key answers `409` `ID_EXISTS` on `holder.id`.

### 9. The bank's PDF upload matches a line one day off and moves it (`95e8fea031ba6bd1`) ⚠️

- `POST /statements/import` matches in two passes: the row's exact day first, over the whole paper, as before; then each row still unmatched, in paper order, takes a line one day before or after it on its account with the same direction and amount, the oldest where several qualify. A line on a row's own day is never lost to a neighbouring row.
- The line is moved to the row's day (00:00 of it in `display_timezone`, every live part), then assigned and placed like any matched line, onto a sealed day or out of one alike, never refusing the upload. Each part moved writes an `UPDATED` entry.
- **Added:** `redated` on every row, `true` when its line was moved (on a dry run, would be), else `false`; `counts.redated`.
- **Removed:** `near_misses`. Before → after, a paper row of 100.00 out on 15 March and your line of 100.00 out on 14 March:
  - before: `"outcome": "new"`, `"bank_line_id": "<a new line>"`, `"near_misses": [{"bank_line_id": "<the 14 March line>", "date": "2026-03-14"}]`, and the close flagged the 14 March line;
  - after: `"outcome": "matched"`, `"bank_line_id": "<the 14 March line>"`, `"redated": true`, that line now on 15 March, nothing flagged.
- A line two or more days off is not matched: the upload creates the paper's row and the close flags the line, as before.

## What the TUI does

- Create people with `POST /holders` (`is_person: true`, `accounts`) or `POST /accounts` on a person holder; stop sending `POST /people`.
- Turn a person into a real account, and back, with `POST /holders/{id}/make-account` and `/make-person` on its holder; stop sending the account routes.
- Archive and unarchive a person with `POST /holders/{id}/archive` and `/unarchive`.

- Read `is_person` on holders. List holders as two groups, bank then person, each ordered by its own `sort_order`.
- Hide the statement, PDF upload and bank download actions for a person, or show `PERSON_TAKES_NO_STATEMENT`.
- Show a line for each new reason: `HOLDER_KIND_MISMATCH` and `PERSON_TAKES_NO_STATEMENT`. Drop `PERSON_NOT_MOVED`, `ARCHIVE_THE_HOLDER` and `HOLDER_HAS_OTHER_ACCOUNTS`, which nothing sends.

- Unpair an account with one `POST /accounts/{id}/move` sending `holder` (section 8); drop the two-write flow and its cleanup of an empty holder. Show a line for the move's new refusals keyed `holder.<field>`.
- After a PDF upload, read `redated` instead of `near_misses`, and show the owner which lines moved to the bank's day (section 9). The twin advice for a flagged line now reads as in section 12.
