## What changed

A statement line (`SIG-202603-001`, the sheet's `COD_EECC`) is no longer typed or sent by
anyone. The engine builds it from three things:

- **The account's statement code**: three capital letters for the bank product (`SIG`, `PEN`,
  `USD`, `ORO`), set on the account. A card's soles and dollar accounts share one code; a person
  never has one.
- **The statement's month**: the month its reconciliation ends. One statement per code per
  month. A card statement is two reconciliations, one per currency, over exactly the same days.
- **The line's place on the paper statement**: the owner puts the statement's lines in the
  paper's order once, both currencies mixed, leaving an empty slot for a paper line the ledger
  doesn't have (contract: PUT /v1/reconciliations/{reconciliation_id}/statement-order).

A line shows its statement line only once its reconciliation is completed. Completing a
reconciliation on an account with a code is refused until every line of the statement has a
place (contract: POST /v1/reconciliations/{reconciliation_id}/complete). Every statement line
stored before is gone.

This client's app already sends no statement line. Only the live contract tests still do, and
7 of them fail against `70476c8797566383`.

## What it must do

From the engine's "What the TUI does":

1. **Catch up with the contract:** the statement code on accounts, the place on transactions and
   bank lines, the two statement-order routes, and every new refusal reason
   (`STATEMENT_CODE_EXISTS`, `HAS_STATEMENT_CODE`, `STATEMENT_MONTH_TAKEN`, `NO_STATEMENT_CODE`,
   `TRAILING_EMPTY_SLOT`, `LINE_NOT_IN_STATEMENT`, `OPENING_BALANCE_NOT_PLACED`,
   `STATEMENT_LINES_MISSING`, `STATEMENT_NOT_ORDERED`, `COMPLETED_STATEMENT_JOINED`).
2. **No test sends a statement line** on a write.
3. **Account form:** a statement-code field, on bank accounts only.
4. **A card's reconciliation as one statement:** its two halves shown together, and both
   completed.
5. **A screen to put a statement in order:** both currencies in one list, empty slots allowed,
   sent once.
6. **Show the statement line** where the owner wants to see it.

The engine asks the client to guide the owner so a card's two halves are always created
together: a half created over the days of a completed one cannot complete until the completed
one is reverted and the whole order is sent again.

## What the owner settled

- **The sheet's `COD_EECC` is ignored at the official import** (owner, 2026-10-24): it only
  groups split rows, as the import already does, and an imported line's statement line stays
  empty until its month is reconciled and ordered in the app ([decisions.md](../decisions.md)
  "The sheet import brings a split purchase in as one split transaction"). No task follows from
  it.

## Found along the way

Not built in this spec; drafted into a new spec when it is done.

- **The statement order's read does not mark the opening balance.** The screen would have to
  match the account's `opening_balance_transaction_id` against each line's parts to keep it out
  of the list; a flag on the read is the complete answer, as an engine ask.
- **`PUT /bank-lines/{id}` refuses `statement_line` and `statement_place` too** (contract
  `58d18ab42f4c09a2`); its pin belongs to the catch-up for that route.
- **No unit guard refuses `statement_line` or `statement_place` in a request body**, as
  `_no_line_code_in_requests` in [tests/unit/conftest.py](../../tests/unit/conftest.py) does for
  `line_code`.
- **[test_cmd_import.py](../../tests/unit/test_cmd_import.py) copies one account record about 25
  times**; one small builder would do.
