A person's opening hint

A person's Edit form (e on Manage ▸ Accounts) has an opening line, as a bank account's does. While you type on it, the line under it explains the sign. Today it is the bank account's words, which speak of money you have; on a person, a positive balance is money they owe you. This page draws the two choices. The names and amounts are invented.

A · Keep the bank account's words

The same hint on every account, a person included.
