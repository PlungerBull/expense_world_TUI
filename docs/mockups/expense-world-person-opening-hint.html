A person's opening hint

A person's Edit form (e on Manage ▸ Accounts) has an opening line, as a bank account's does. Today a long line under it explains the sign while you type. This page draws your idea: no line under it, and on the right of the OPENING line one word, debt or credit, that follows the sign as you type, then the currency. The names and amounts are invented.

On a person, -150.00 is money you owe them (your debt), 150.00 money they owe you (your credit). Two small things are left to pick, at the bottom.

1 · typing a negative amount on a person — your idea

The word on the right says debt; the line under it is gone.

3 · question a · the line empty, or 0.00

Before you type, or at zero, there is no debt or credit. Recommended: the currency alone, so it is always there.

4 · question b · a bank account's opening line

A card's opening balance is negative when you owe the bank. Recommended: the same word on every account, so one sign reads one way everywhere; here, the Visa's soles account.

The other choice keeps today's line under it on a bank account, and the word only on a person. A savings account's positive balance would read credit.

To pick: a · when the line is empty or 0.00: the currency alone (recommended), or nothing. b · on a bank account too (recommended), or on a person only.
