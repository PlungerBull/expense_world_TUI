Accounts and their currencies · six picks

An account now holds one or more currencies, soles (PEN) and dollars (USD), each with its own balance and its own opening balance, never added together. A person is an account too. This page decides how Manage ▸ Accounts, Home, the New and Edit forms show that, and how a currency is added and removed: questions 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 of catch-up-currencies-6-main-views.md.

Each drawing is today's screen, changed only by the option it shows. Where an option depends on question 1, it is drawn under 1 B, the recommended one. Every name and amount is made up: the bank accounts Banco Inventado (PEN, USD), Visa Inventada (PEN, USD, statement code VIN), Caja Chica (PEN), Cuenta Prueba (PEN, nothing in it) and the archived Ahorro Viejo (PEN, USD); the people Ana Ejemplo, Rosa Modelo and Luis Ficticio, each in PEN and USD.

Pick one letter per question; the answer list is at the bottom. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
