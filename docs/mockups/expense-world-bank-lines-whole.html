A purchase, whole · reconciling and deleting a split purchase

A purchase split into parts is still one charge on the bank statement: FARMACIA, -100.00, split into -60.00 of medicines (SALUD) and -40.00 of cleaning things (HOGAR). The ledger keeps one row per part. The reconciliation screens show the purchase once, with its total, and d on any part deletes the whole purchase.

Part 2 is the reconciliation screens, part 3 is d. Each drawing uses the app's real words and layout; only the new words are new. The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

2a · A month still open, with a split purchase — which bank columns show

The Octubre 2026 reconciliation, still a draft. Every row shows only what the bank statement shows, one line each, with no category, hashtags or note. FARMACIA is one row with its total, -100.00, and its title alone: nothing says it is split. Its parts are not listed here. The cursor is on FARMACIA, which is not ticked yet. The choice is which bank columns show. The engine gives each bank line its date, title, total and the bank's operation number. Nothing else on it is the bank's, so there are two options.
