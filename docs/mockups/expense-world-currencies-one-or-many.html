One currency or several · three picks

Your rule: an account holding one currency shows no currency picker, the currency is just shown; an account holding several makes you pick one.

The LOG bar already works this way as you picked it (8 A and 8·i). The pages you picked from drew two places differently: the Edit form (question 9 A) stops on a currency picker even when the account holds one currency, and moving selected rows with b (question 11 A) keeps each row's currency. This page draws those places under your rule, as questions 12 to 14.

Every drawing uses made-up accounts: Banco Inventado (PEN and USD), Caja Chica (PEN only) and Visa Inventada (PEN and USD). Today is Saturday 31 October 2026. Red refusal lines stand for the engine's own words; the sentences drawn are illustrative. Purple marks a currency that changed. Moving a row to another currency never converts its amount: -23.00 in dollars becomes -23.00 in soles.

Pick one letter per question. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
