Statements · the ? card on the "no statement" list

On the Statements screen, u lists an account's bank lines in no statement, and ? opens the card of keys. Today the card lists keys that do nothing on that list (esc Close the panel, n, u, both pgdn rows and pgup once every row is loaded), shows esc Back twice, and on a terminal under 30 rows its last lines are cut.

Every pane is the card at 80 columns over an invented account. The words of each row are the app's own; only which rows show changes. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
