First-time setup, inside the app

On a Mac where the app has never run, it needs two things before it can show anything: where the engine is (its address) and the key that lets it in. Today both have to be typed as a command before the app will start. These screens ask for them inside the app.

Screens 1, 2 and 7 each have a choice to make, with the options drawn side by side or one under the other and the recommended one marked. Screens 3 to 6 have no choice; they show what follows from screen 1 A. Nothing is built until you pick. The address, key and names are invented.

1 · What you see the first time — the app starts with nothing saved on this Mac

Today this moment is a dead end: Home opens, says it cannot load, and tells you to type a command. Three ways to replace it. In all three the app opens with expense alone and nothing has to be typed outside it.

3 · The key, and the check — one drawing, no choice to make

5 · A key the engine has never seen — asked only when it is needed

If the engine accepts the key but has no profile for it yet (a brand-new engine), one more question appears: your name. That replaces today's detour to System ▸ Auth & profile and b. The time zone is read from this Mac and shown, not asked. When the engine already knows you, this screen never appears and you go straight to screen 6.

6 · Connected — you land on Home

The moment the engine answers (and your profile exists), Setup closes and Home opens as on any other day, with a short note in the bottom-right corner. From here on the app starts on Home; Setup shows again only if the saved settings are gone. Changing the address or the key later stays where it is today, on System ▸ Config.
