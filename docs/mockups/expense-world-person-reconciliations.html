Turning an account into a person: refused for its reconciliations

The engine now has one more reason to refuse t on Manage ▸ Accounts: a person never has reconciliations, so a bank account with one, a draft or a completed one, cannot become a person until its reconciliations are deleted. Nothing changes, and once they are gone t works.

The refusal is the engine's real words, shown whole as every refusal is. The only question is whether the app adds a line saying how, the way it does for an opening balance (Press e and empty its opening balance first.). The accounts and amounts are invented.

1 · When the engine refuses — the cursor on ALEJO PEN, a bank account with a reconciliation · B picked

The engine's message already says to delete its reconciliations first. What it can't say is how in this app: on the Reconciliations screen, d deletes a draft, and a completed one must first go back to draft with u.
