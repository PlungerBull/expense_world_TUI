Wording picks

Six small questions about what the app says: a key card, three hints under a form's bar, a note when a date can't be read, a note when a bulk edit is refused, and one about a pop-up that should be a full screen. Each question shows the screen as it is today first, then each option as the screen would look.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

Question 1 · the ? card on a split transaction — not picked: see the split transaction screen · Reconciliations ▸ an account ▸ u “no batch” ▸ ?

On Reconciliations you open Sueldo Soles and press u: its rows that are in no batch. The cursor is on TOTTUS JOCKEY PLAZA -100.00, a transaction split into two parts (-60.00 and -40.00). You press ?. The card says ⏎ lets you edit “its date, title, operation, amount or account”. But on a split transaction ⏎ opens a form with no amount line: the parts' amounts are shown read-only, and you change them by splitting it again in the ledger. On UBER *TRIP, never split, ⏎ does offer the amount.
