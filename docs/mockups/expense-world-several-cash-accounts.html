Several cash accounts

Cash is no longer one account named Efectivo. You can make as many as you like (your wallet, a jar at home, dollars kept apart), and the engine names each one in order: CASH-0001, CASH-0002, … Your Efectivo is already CASH-0001, with the same balances and transactions. A number is never reused, and a cash account with no transactions can now be deleted.

Accounts and amounts are invented. Drawn at 80 columns.

n on Manage ▸ Accounts. cash is now always offered on the type line, however many you hold. Until the engine answers, the app can't know which number the new one gets, so the form can no longer show a name.

3 · Deleting a cash account — no pick: what the engine now allows

d comes back on a cash row and works as on any account's. The engine deletes a cash account only while it holds no transaction (its opening balance counts as one). Otherwise it refuses, and the app shows its words. Archive stays the way to put away one that holds history.

4 · Cash on the LOG bar — no pick: no change in the app, but you'll notice it

$ finds a cash account by its name, as any account. With one cash account, $cash lands on it. With two or more it matches all of them and asks you to be precise, as $bcp does with two BCP cards. $efectivo no longer finds anything: that name is gone.

expense · + on Home, two cash accounts
  LOG  -25 almuerzo $cash▍
  "$cash" matches 2 accounts

  LOG  -25 almuerzo $cash-0002▍
  -25.00 · almuerzo · CASH-0002 · today

  LOG  -25 almuerzo $efectivo▍
  "$efectivo" matches no account

The LOG bar's other lines are left out. A shorter way to name one cash account (say $c2) would be a new rule in the app's grammar: say so if you want it, and it becomes its own question.

To pick: one letter per pane with a question, e.g. 1A 2A. Panes 3 and 4 are drawn for you to object to, not to pick.
