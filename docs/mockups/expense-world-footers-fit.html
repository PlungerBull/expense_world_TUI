Footers · a footer too wide for the screen

From page 2 of a list on, the footer gains pgup Prev, and on some screens it no longer fits the terminal, so its last key is cut. The Statements screens already avoid this by tightening the footer's spacing when it would not fit.

Every screen is drawn by the app itself, with invented hashtags and transactions. Option B's footers are the one part drawn by hand. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
