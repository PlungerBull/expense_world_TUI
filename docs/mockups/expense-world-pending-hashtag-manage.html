Built-in rows · Manage ▸ Hashtags and Categories, and opening balances

The built-in pending hashtag, #Pendiente, on the screen where hashtags are managed: it is marked, and it can be neither renamed nor deleted; no other hashtag may take its name. The built-in categories @Pendiente and @Opening follow the same rule on Manage ▸ Categories: they are system values, so they keep their names and stay. And an opening balance, the one transaction that carries no hashtag at all. Home and tagging are in their own mockup.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · The pending hashtag on Manage ▸ Hashtags — a table like Categories — your ask, 2026-10-17

Hashtags become a table with the same System column as Categories: system 🔒 on the built-in row, — on the rest, and the same kind of legend under the list. Hashtags are listed in the engine's order. 4 · The two refusals — SYSTEM_HASHTAG and RESERVED_NAME

Deleting the pending hashtag is refused by the engine (SYSTEM_HASHTAG). With d gone from its row you would only meet it in a rare case, such as another client sending the delete, or a list read before the engine made the hashtag. Naming a hashtag Pendiente, new or renamed, in any letter case, is refused too (RESERVED_NAME): the name belongs to the pending hashtag. Both engine sentences are stand-ins. As with the other refusals, the question is whether a line of the app's follows the engine's words.
