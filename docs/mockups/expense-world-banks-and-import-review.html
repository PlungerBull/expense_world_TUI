Banks, and the import's review

Drawn for the engine's contract 3.1: a bank is a 3–4 letter code with a required last 4, and the sheet import suggests the accounts it is missing and makes the ones you confirm, with the names and rows, in one write. Nothing here is built.

Panes 1 and 2 draw what is decided; 3 B, 4 A, 5 A, 6 A and 7 B are picked. Answer panes 8, 9, 10 and 11 with one letter each, e.g. 8A 9A 10A 11A. Accounts, numbers, amounts and the engine's words are invented. Drawn at 80 columns.

The bank account form · decided

Manage ▸ Accounts ▸ New. A bank account is a code of 3 or 4 letters and its last 4, named CODE-1234 by the engine; no bank has to exist first and nothing is suggested. Only a bank whose statements the app reads (today BCP) asks for a statement code. People and cash keep the names you type. Panes 1 and 2 draw this, to check; they ask no letter.

The statement code line appears once BANK is a code the engine reads statements for, and leaves again if BANK changes to one it doesn't. On the Edit form a number is removed only while another stays: a bank account always has its last 4.

The import's review

The ledger is empty. The engine suggests an account for each sheet name it doesn't know, and the review makes them, the categories, the hashtags and the rows in one write. It guesses only from what a name says: a name starting BCP gets a BCP bank account, with its last 4 only when the name holds four digits; VISA Infinite Sapphire names no bank and ALEJO no kind, so those you set once, here. On your sheet, the engine says the five BCP … names get BCP with no last 4, and the other 13 get nothing.
