The bank account form · decided

Manage ▸ Accounts ▸ New. A bank account is a code of 3 or 4 letters and its last 4, named CODE-1234 by the engine; no bank has to exist first and nothing is suggested. Only a bank whose statements the app reads (today BCP) asks for a statement code. People and cash keep the names you type. Panes 1 and 2 draw this, to check; they ask no letter.

The import's review

The ledger is empty. The engine suggests an account for each sheet name it doesn't know, and the review makes them, the categories, the hashtags and the rows in one write. It guesses only from what a name says: a name holding BCP and four digits gets a BCP account with that last 4; VISA Infinite Sapphire names no bank and ALEJO no kind, so those you set once, here.
