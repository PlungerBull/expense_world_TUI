2 · the "may be logged twice" warning

When you picked this warning (2026-09-12, option D) it meant "the ledger holds more rows of this day and amount than the file". The engine's version means something wider: the ledger has a row on this day and amount that no row of the file matched, including when the counts are equal. Two cases, both in this file:

  • Equal counts. The file's CINE PLAZA LIMA PE -28.00 on 09-10 went in as new, and the ledger already has Cinema -28.00 that day, typed in your own words. It is probably the same movement, and --apply would log it twice.
  • More in the ledger. The file has one -3.50 on 09-07 and the ledger has two. One is matched, and the other was probably logged twice already.

Today's text says "ledger 1, file 1 — a row may be logged twice", which reads as a contradiction. B and C say what is true, and count the unmatched ledger rows, not the ledger's total. No ledger titles and no marks on the new rows: whether this grows into "rename your row to the bank's wording?" is still your open question, and it would be an engine ask.
