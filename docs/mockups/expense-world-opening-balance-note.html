The create form's note on the opening balance

An account's opening balance is a line on the account, the first one. When a statement holds that line, the engine already counts it in the statement's sum. So if you also type the paper's starting figure as the statement's begin balance, the money counts twice and the statement never adds up. That statement begins at 0.00.

The form cannot know which statement will hold the opening balance, so the note is always there under BEGIN BALANCE, on the one-account form and on each half of a card's form. Nothing else on the form changes. Pick one wording.

The accounts and amounts are invented. Words that are new are listed under Pick.
