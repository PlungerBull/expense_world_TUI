Split · the screen

s on a ledger row opens this screen. One bank charge becomes several parts: Tottus Jockey Plaza -100.00 becomes -60.00 of food and -40.00 of cleaning products. The title is the transaction's, so it is one field on top and changing it changes every part. The date and the account are the bank's, shown and never asked. Each part has an amount, a category, hashtags and a note, each in its own field, with the same suggestion list the Edit form has. No typed line, no grammar.

Every pane shows the same moment: part 1 is filled in, and on part 2 you have typed ho in the category, so the list offers HOGAR and HOSPEDAJE. The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

Already picked

esc leaves at once, nothing sent. A category or hashtag field shows the suggestion list as you type. A new title reaches every part, sent with the split in one write (asked of the engine).

Question for the owner

  1. Pick one layout: A, B or C.
    • A · a table you edit in place: every part is a row, every field a cell; arrows move from cell to cell, as in a spreadsheet. Compact: ten parts still fit on one screen.
    • B · the Edit form, part after part (recommended): what you described. One field per line, ↑↓ moves through them, from the title down through part 1, part 2 and + add a part. It works exactly like the Edit form you already use, the same keys and the same suggestion list.
    • C · the parts on the left, the picked part's fields on the right: ↑↓ on the left picks a part, ⇥ goes into its fields. Short list, and only one part's fields at a time.
