Split · the screen

s on a ledger row opens this screen: Tottus Jockey Plaza -100.00 becomes -60.00 of food and -40.00 of cleaning products. On top, a table of the parts that the cursor moves through. ⏎ on a part opens its fields in a section below, one per line, like the Edit form: amount, category, hashtags, note. The title is the transaction's: one line above the table, edited the same way, and it changes every part. The date and the account are never asked.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

Already picked

The layout: your table on top, the picked part's fields below. esc on the table leaves at once, nothing sent. A category or hashtag field shows the suggestion list as you type. A new title reaches every part, sent with the split in one write (asked of the engine).

Questions for the owner

  1. After the last field. ⏎ on NOTE:
    • A (recommended): back to the table, the cursor on the part you just edited, the section below closed.
    • B: straight on to the next part's AMOUNT, so you can fill every part without going back.
  2. esc in the section below.
    • A (recommended): back to the table, keeping every field you already moved past with ⏎ or ↑↓; only the field you were typing in goes back to what it was.
    • B: back to the table, and the whole part goes back to what it was before ⏎ opened it.
  3. Agree with the four panes as drawn, or say what differs.
