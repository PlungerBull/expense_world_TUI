Setup hint · what the app says when first-time setup is missing

When the engine answers that first-time setup has not been done, the app adds one hint line under the engine's error. The hint is the only part the app writes; the lines above it are the engine's own and are drawn here with a stand-in sentence. Not approved. Variant A is what the app shows today.

Each variant is drawn twice: as the toast in expense world at 80 columns, and as the output of a typed command.
