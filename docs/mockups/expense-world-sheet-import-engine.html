Sheet import · through the engine

expense import sheet sends your spreadsheet to the engine whole; the engine reads it and answers. This is what the terminal prints. Every name, row and amount here is made up; the refusal words stand in for the engine's own, which the app shows as the engine writes them.

As you answered: a name you don't have yet is created by the app on --apply; running again with --apply is your confirmation; --account-name stays and is remembered; an account used in two currencies is two accounts with the same name.

Pick one letter for screen 1, and say yes or no to screens 2 and 3. The button at the top right shows a light terminal.

Screen 3 · a sheet with bad rows — dry run or apply
One bad row stops the whole import. Every refusal is listed, in the engine's words; rows refused alike share one line. On --apply the check runs before any name is created, so a refused sheet leaves nothing behind.
