Edit · a part of a split purchase

The Edit form (⏎ on a ledger row) on one part of a split purchase. What is new: it says which changes reach every part, and a refusal caused by another part says which part. The split and the ledger have their own mockups.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · Editing one part — ⏎ on Tottus's first part: -60.00, COMIDA, verduras

A part's amount, category, hashtags and note are its own. Its date, title and account are the purchase's: change one of them here and the engine writes it on every part, in one go. The form says so before you save. On a row that is not split, the form is exactly as today.

2 · A refusal caused by the other part — the engine names the part by its id

You move Tottus's first part to 2026-10-10. Its second part (-40.00, HOGAR) is in a completed reconciliation, so its date is locked, and the engine refuses the whole change: nothing is written on either part. Its message names the part that caused it, by the part's id. The sentence is the engine's real wording; the first line is a stand-in. The form stays open with what you typed.
