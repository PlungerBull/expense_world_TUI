Home as the Ledger

Home stops being a menu with a stats line and becomes the month itself: every category with what it holds this month, every bank account with its balance, every person with what is owed, then Manage and System. Enter on a category or an account opens the ledger filtered to it. On any filtered ledger, space selects rows and one key changes the category, the account or the hashtags of the whole selection. Title, description, amount and date stay single-row edits behind ⏎.

Drawn with your real September figures on 2026-09-12: the only rows this month are the two bank imports, so @Pendiente holds the whole month and every other category reads zero — which is exactly the state the categorising pass starts from. No suggestions, no automatic spreading across a merchant: keeping it simple first.

What changes

  • Home — the menu rows for Inbox, Transactions and Overview's band are replaced by live rows. The stats line goes: its numbers are now rows. One fetch, the same /dashboard Home already reads.
  • Transactions — gains a filter crumb (category, account or hashtag), row selection with space, and three bulk keys: c category, b bank account, h hashtags. The p key from the pendiente work is retired — @Pendiente is a category row on Home now.
  • Overview stays as it is — the four-month grid is history; Home is the present.
  • Engine — nothing. Bulk is one PUT per selected row through the existing write queue; the filters are the list endpoint's existing ones.

Two things this mockup leaves for you to pick

  • Pane 1 — accounts show the native balance only (A), or native with the PEN equivalent for USD accounts (B).
  • Pane 6 — after a bulk change on a filtered list, the changed rows leave the list immediately (C) or stay until you refresh (D).

C is the recommendation. The list is the work queue; a row you have dealt with should be out of the way, and the count in the legend should be the count that is left. The toast is the receipt. D keeps the receipt on screen longer at the cost of a list that lies about its own filter.

keys · on a filtered or unfiltered ledger
spaceselect / unselect the cursor row. The cursor does not move — ↓ then space is the rhythm, as in every file manager.
aselect every row on the page, or clear the selection when everything is selected.
c · b · hcategory · bank account · hashtags, for the selection — or for the cursor row when nothing is selected. Each opens the picker the edit form already uses; ↵ applies, esc cancels.
↵the edit form for the cursor row, unchanged — title, description, amount, date, and the same three fields one row at a time.
escclears the selection if there is one; else clears the filter; else leaves the screen — one key, three retreats, never a surprise.
what bulk meansone PUT /transactions/{id} per selected row through the write queue, in order, then one reload. The receipt is a toast: 3 rows → KORAKUEN. A refused row (a locked account on a completed reconciliation, a category deleted meanwhile) is named by row; the others go through.
what stays singletitle, description, amount, date — the four fields that identify a row, and the four a reconciliation locks. Editing them in bulk would be the tool for creating mistakes at scale.
rejected · the ones that needed more than a line
