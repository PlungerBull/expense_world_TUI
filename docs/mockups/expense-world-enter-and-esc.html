Enter and esc on the forms

Two small questions about what a key does. Question 1 is the Edit form (⏎ on a ledger row): what ⏎ does on the ACCOUNT or CATEGORY line when you type nothing. Question 2 is the split screen (s on a ledger row): what esc does to the hashtags you just added on a part.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

Question 3 · CATEGORY on a transaction still in @Pendiente — picked: A

With 1 A, landing on CATEGORY highlights the category the transaction has. But a transaction still waiting for a category is in @Pendiente, and today the Edit form's category list never shows @Pendiente (the bulk c pick does). So there is nothing to highlight. Uber a casa -18.50 is in @Pendiente; you step down to CATEGORY.
