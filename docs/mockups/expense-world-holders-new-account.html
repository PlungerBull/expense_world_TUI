How a new bank account gets its card

Every bank account sits on a card, and a card holds at most one account per currency. So making a bank account now also means saying which card it is on. The example is the Visa: its soles account Visa Signature PEN exists, on the card Visa Signature (code SIG), and its dollar account is being made. Option C also shows the Visa made from nothing.

A person is on no card and is made as today. The screens are drawn as if Manage ▸ Accounts' Code column has become Card (question 1 A). The accounts and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

One write, and the dollar account is on the Visa's card at once. The Visa itself, made from nothing, is two trips through the form: the soles account on a new card, then the dollar account picking it. Pairing afterwards is for an account put on the wrong card.

The name is the card's; each account takes it with its currency after it. The statement code is typed once, for the card. One write makes the card and both accounts, or nothing at all. A savings account is the same form with one currency: Sueldo Soles, PEN, one account named Sueldo Soles.

A refusal names the account it is about, the engine's way (accounts.1 is the second, the dollar one), and nothing is created. The words here are invented; the screen shows the engine's.

Pick A, B or C. Recommended C: a card of two currencies is one form and one write, and its code is typed once; nothing to pair afterwards. An account added later to a card that exists, as the Visa gaining a third currency, is made on a card of its own and paired onto it.
