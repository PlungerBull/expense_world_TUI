Why a line is read-only, and three notes

The screens for the spec Notes that keep the engine's words, and lines that say they are locked. Each screen shows the app as it is today first, then each option. The text that changes is in blue, and what goes away is in red.

The rows are invented. The reasons beside read-only are the engine's sentences, word for word: the app shows what the engine sends. The terminal is 80 columns wide. Use the button at the top right to see a light terminal.

1 · Why a line is read-only, on the Edit form — Capture & ledger ▸ Transactions ▸ ⏎

You open Farmacia, a transaction in a completed reconciliation. Its date, title and account can't change, and neither can its amount while it has one part. Today each of those lines says read-only and nothing more. The engine now sends a sentence with each one, and these options show where it goes. The second row of drawings is an opening balance, whose three locked lines each have a different sentence. An archived account's transaction reads like the first row, with the sentence “The account is archived. Unarchive it first.”
