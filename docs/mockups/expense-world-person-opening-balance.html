A person's opening balance

Since 2026-10-27 the engine lets a person carry an opening balance, as a bank account does: what they already owed you, or you them, on the day you started tracking them. Turning an account that has an opening balance into a person keeps it, with the same amount and date.

Today a person's Edit form (e on Manage ▸ Accounts) has no opening and as of lines, and t Make person on an account with an opening balance is refused, with the app adding Press e and empty its opening balance first. This page draws the two choices for the form. The names and amounts are invented.

A · A person's Edit form takes an opening balance — recommendednew

The same two lines a bank account's Edit form has, after the colour, both optional: the amount, signed (-150.00 is money you owe them, 150.00 money they owe you), and its date. A person never has a statement code, so that line stays off.

B · A person's Edit form stays as it is

No opening lines on a person. A person gets an opening balance only from the sheet import (a person's first SALDO INICIAL row), or by being turned into a person after it was set on the bank account. t still turns an account with one, as in A3 and A4.
