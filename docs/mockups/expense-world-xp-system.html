Expense World XP · the whole system in the TUI

Sketch for productivity_world/expense-world-xp-system.md. Not approved and nothing is built. It shows where each piece lives and what you see when it happens, in the order a normal month runs. Every amount, name and XP price is invented. XP prices are placeholders until the pricing question is settled.

Four surfaces: a strip on Home (always on), toasts when something is earned, a chapter recap once a month, and a Character screen for the full picture. Plus a one-time setup: a role on each account and on some categories.

Always on

5 · Complete a card reconciliation — the card boss, one fight per statement cycle

Completing a reconciliation on a credit account resolves that cycle's fight. You win if the cycle holds no row in a penalty category (interest, fees), meaning the statement was paid in full. A lost fight still pays for reconciling. It never takes XP away. Reverting the reconciliation withdraws what it paid.

6 · Set what your investments are worth — about once a month

Opened from Character (u) or from an investing account. You type today's value. The engine records the difference as returns. Everything else that entered the account since the last value counts as a contribution. XP is paid only on new ground: after a dip, it starts again once the loss is recovered.

7 · The chapter recap — the first time you open the TUI in a new month

A one-time card over Home that closes last month. It tells the month as a story: what you earned, how the Shield moved, whether you levelled up. Months with nothing to show for a line just leave that line out. ⏎ dismisses it. Past chapters stay on the Character screen.

One-time setup
9 · Give accounts and categories a role — a new column on two existing screens

The system rests on two markings. An account role decides which accounts feed the Shield (savings), Wealth (investing) and card cycles (credit). A category role tells moves between your own accounts apart from spending (move), lets bills not break a no-spend day (fixed), and marks interest and fees (penalty). r opens a small picker. Most rows keep the default.

What this sketch decides, and what it leaves open
  1. Placement (for review): strip on Home, toasts, monthly recap, Character under a new PROGRESS section, roles as a column on Accounts and Categories.
  2. Never shown: XP going down, red penalties, streak warnings, reminders to log by hand.
  3. Placeholders: every XP amount, the medal thresholds, the recap trigger (first open of a new month), where investing values are set.
  4. Open (in the design doc): XP pricing, one level or per-pillar levels, the level curve, whether past history earns XP.
