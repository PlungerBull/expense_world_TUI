1 · The staged list at 80 columns — three lines staged, one on an account called BCP Signature PEN

When the rows are wider than the screen, some columns have to be cut short. Today every column shrinks a little, so the amount is cut and the currency disappears. Both options below keep the date, amount, currency and account whole. They differ only in which of the text columns are cut. A cut cell ends in …; the whole row shows again when you pick it with ↑↓ and it comes back to the bar.

2 · The OPERATION line's note — Reconciliations ▸ u No batch ▸ ⏎ on a line split into two parts

On a line split into two or more parts, the date, title, account and currency lines already say changes every part of the transaction under the bar, because the engine writes them on every part. The bank's operation number belongs to the whole line in the same way, but its line does not say so. A line with one part shows no note on any line, whatever you pick.

A · says it, like date, title, account and currencyrecommended
