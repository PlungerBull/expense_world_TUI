Ledger · the parts of one purchase, together

A purchase can now be split into parts (the split screen has its own mockup): one bank charge of -100.00 at Tottus can be -60.00 of COMIDA and -40.00 of HOGAR. This page is how the ledger shows those parts: together, under the purchase's title and total.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · The whole ledger — one line per purchase, its parts inside — your ask, 2026-10-17

A split purchase is one line in the ledger: its title, total, date and account, and a mark that it is split. Its parts show only when you open it. Every other row looks exactly as today. A screen for the purchase: its parts in a list. ⏎ on a part opens that part's Edit form (the edit-a-part mockup, with its every part hint on date, title and account); s opens the split screen with these parts. Recommended: you edit one part like any transaction, and splitting again is one key away.

The ledger's s Split on the line opens the split screen directly, as on any row. In a ledger filtered from Home each part is its own plain row (screen 2, A, your pick), so s on a part there opens the same split screen with all the purchase's parts.
