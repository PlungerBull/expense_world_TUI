Ledger · the parts of one purchase, together

A purchase can now be split into parts (the split screen has its own mockup): one bank charge of -100.00 at Tottus can be -60.00 of COMIDA and -40.00 of HOGAR. This page is how the ledger shows those parts: together, under the purchase's title and total.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · The whole ledger — a purchase in parts, a single row, the cursor and a selection

Two purchases are split: Tottus Jockey Plaza, -100.00 in two parts, and Plaza Vea, -235.40 in three. Every other row is one purchase in one row and looks exactly as today. A part has its own note (the Description column), amount, category and hashtags; the title, date and account are the purchase's, so they are written once, on the purchase's line. The ledger keeps its newest-first order: the parts of one purchase have the same date and sit together.

One more question, beyond the pick: the purchase's total and its 2 parts are the sum and count of the purchase's parts. Today the engine says only which purchase each row belongs to, so the app would add up the parts it has loaded, and when a page ends between two parts it would have to read on until it has them all. Should we ask the engine to send each row's purchase total and number of parts (and to list one purchase's parts on request), recommended, so no app ever adds them up itself? Or should the app add up what it loads?
