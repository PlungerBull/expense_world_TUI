t on a person needs a card

t on Manage ▸ Accounts turns a person into a bank account, keeping its name, balance and transactions. Every bank account now sits on a card, so the person needs one to go on. Here the person ALEJO USD becomes a bank account.

Drawn with the Card column of question 1 A. After any write the list reads again from its first page, cursor on row 1. The accounts and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

t opens the question, the way the import door asks which account a file is. The list holds every card with no account in the person's currency, then a new card named after the person. ⏎ turns it; esc leaves it a person. Drawn after picking Sueldo Soles.

Pick A or B. Recommended B, as question 2 B: you put the account where it belongs in one step, and a person in two currencies never collides on a card name.
