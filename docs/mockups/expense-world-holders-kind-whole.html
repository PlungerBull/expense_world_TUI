t turns the whole holder

t on Manage ▸ Accounts turns a bank account into a person, or a person into a bank account. The engine now puts people on holders, as it does bank accounts, and turns a whole holder at once: every account on it changes kind together. A holder of one account works exactly as t does today.

So t on Visa Signature PEN also turns Visa Signature USD, which sits on the same holder. The question is what you see when one key turns two accounts. The refusal at the bottom is the same whichever you pick. The names and amounts are invented; the refusal's words are the engine's.
