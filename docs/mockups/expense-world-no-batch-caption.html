Reconciliations · the “no batch” caption

The “no batch” list (u on Reconciliations) marks with ! a line in no statement whose day falls inside one of the account's statements, and its caption explains the mark as ! blocks a batch's close. That is no longer true: closing a statement now goes through and flags such a line for review instead. Only the caption's words change; the rows and the mark stay. The rows are invented.
