Statements · a row that is not in order, at 80 columns

On an account with a statement code, the list of statements ends with an Order column: in order, or not in order when the statement's lines are not yet in the paper's order. The approved screen draws only in order rows. A not in order row needs 82 columns, and the terminal has 80, so something has to give. Which one?

The accounts and amounts are invented. Everything else on the screen is as approved.
