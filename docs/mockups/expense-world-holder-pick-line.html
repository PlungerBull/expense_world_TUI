A holder of three currencies on the import door's account list

The engine lets a holder hold one account per currency, so three or more is possible. Here the holder Interbank Global holds three accounts the owner named Sueldo (PEN), Viajes (USD) and Euros (EUR). A card's two accounts, BCP Signature PEN and BCP Signature USD, show as today in both panes. No such holder is in the owner's data today. The names are invented.
