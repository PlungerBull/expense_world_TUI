Home absorbs Overview

Since 2026-09-12 Home shows every category this month and every balance today. Overview shows the same accounts in its band and the same month as its grid's newest column. Two screens draw one set of numbers twice. What Overview has that Home lacks is short: the three months before this one, the hashtag rows under each category, inflow and outflow, and pgup / pgdn to walk back in time.

These panes bring those four things onto Home and delete overview.py. Drawn with your real June–September figures on 2026-09-13. Categories stay in PEN; balances stay in their own currency, as picked yesterday. Nothing here needs the engine to change.

What Overview has, and where it goes

accounts bandAlready on Home, uncapped, with ↵ into the ledger. The band's 5-row cap goes with it.
people band + settled foldAlready on Home. The ▶ 3 settled fold moves onto the PEOPLE header when anyone is settled.
newest grid columnAlready on Home as this month.
3 earlier monthsMoves to Home in option A; one earlier month in option B.
hashtag rows ▶/▼Moves to Home. → unfolds a category, ← folds it, as on Overview today.
inflow · outflow · netMoves to Home under the categories; Home's lone net line becomes these three.
pgup / pgdn windowMoves to Home. Balances stay today's while the months walk back, so the header says balance today, as the band's title did.
flat CLIUnchanged. expense dashboard and expense reports monthly still print what they print.

5 · the pieces to pick

Q1 · Shape — A four months, or B this month and last?

A recommended. It is Overview's grid unchanged, so nothing you read there today goes away.

Q2 · ↵ on a category — C every month, or D the highlighted month?

C recommended for now. D can come later without undoing C.

Q3 · Categories with nothing in the window — keep them, or hide them as the grid does?

Keep them recommended. Home is also the list of places a row can be sent; a hidden HOGAR is a category you cannot open.

Q4 · Delete Overview outright, or keep it one release behind the LEDGER door?

Delete recommended. Every block it draws would be on Home, and two screens of the same numbers is the problem being fixed.
