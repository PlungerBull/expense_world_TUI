Deleting an account a statement holds

A statement holds a balance for every account of its card, even one with no transaction in it: the Visa's dollar account in a month with no dollar spending. While a statement holds it, draft or completed, the engine refuses to delete the account and writes nothing. Here d on Visa Signature USD (no transactions, balance 0.00) is asked and confirmed with y as today, and the engine says no: the November statement SIG-202611, a draft, holds it.

The engine's words are its real ones, shown whole as every refusal in the app is. The question is what the app adds. The statement names and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.
