Statements · the screens, in the account's words

The Statements screens. A statement is now the account's: one name, one set of days, one status, and a starting and an ending balance for each currency it covers. One bank account holds its currencies (the Visa holds soles and dollars); there are no cards or holders as separate things.

Every choice here is already settled: your answers to questions 1 to 5 of the spec, and the earlier picks of the card-statement and PDF questions. Each pane says which answer it follows. You are asked only one thing: do these screens look right?

Every terminal is drawn at 80 columns. The accounts, lines and amounts are invented: Visa BCP holds PEN and USD, statement code VIS; IBK Visa holds PEN and USD, no statement code; Sueldo Soles holds PEN; BBVA Dólares holds USD. A card's balances are signed as the paper shows them: negative is what you owe. Grey refusal and note sentences stand in for the engine's own words. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
