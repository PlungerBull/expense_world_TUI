The statement's screen

One screen for a month's statement: you tick the lines in, put them in the paper's order, and complete the statement, all here. It replaces the separate statement-order screen (task 6), and screen 3, the statement's screen, of expense-world-card-statement.html (task 7). There is no separate order screen and no key o. One row per statement in the list (screen 1 A there) and the one form that creates both halves (screen 2 A there) stay as they are and are not drawn again.

The screen reads like the paper statement: one list, both currencies mixed, with an amount column for each currency, Amount PEN and Amount USD. The first row is the starting balance, soles in the PEN column and dollars in the USD column. Each line below has an amount in one of the two columns, never both. The lines sit in the paper's order, and you move a line with shift+↑↓, as picked for moving. The engine gives the list already in this order, both halves of the card together: first the lines that have their place, in that order, then the lines with no place yet, oldest first. ctrl+s saves the whole order in one save.

Kept as settled: no screen shows the statement line itself (SIG-202610-004) or a place number; a paper line the ledger lacks comes in from the bank's file through the import door, so this screen creates nothing and has no empty slots; a line ticked after the order was saved waits at the end, marked new. The header keeps each half's begin, end, diff and status, and the order line from the engine's counts. c completes both halves, u reverts both, d deletes both, each after its question; space ticks a line in or out of its own half; esc goes back.

The card is Signature PEN and Signature USD, statement code SIG, statement Octubre 2026, 2026-09-26 → 2026-10-25, with the same nine invented lines as the card-statement drawings. Picked on 2026-10-25: question 1 A, 2 A, 3 A, 4 A and 5 A, and panes 1, 6 and 7 as drawn; every screen here is drawn with those answers, except the variants that were not picked. Card balances are signed as the paper shows them: negative is what you owe. The engine's words are its real ones. The button at the top right switches to a light terminal.
