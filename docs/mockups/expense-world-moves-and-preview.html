Moves and the upload preview

Two screens change with the engine's new version. A move into another currency: moving a transaction to an account that holds only soles now turns a dollar line into a soles line, the amount as it is, never converted. The upload preview: before you press ⏎, it now lists the lines the close will flag for review: lines you logged on the paper's days that no statement holds.

Everything else on these screens stays as you know it. Accounts, lines and amounts are invented, and so are the engine's words. Drawn at 80 columns.
