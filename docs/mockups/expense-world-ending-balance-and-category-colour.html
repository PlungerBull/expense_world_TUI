Ending balance at complete · category colour optional

Two changes the engine's update brings to what you see. Part 1: completing a reconciliation that has no ending balance. The engine now refuses that, so the app asks you for the balance first. There are two ways to ask, A and B; pick one. Part 2: a new category no longer needs a colour.

The accounts, names and amounts are invented. The engine's words in the notes are stand-ins until they are read from the engine.

Part 1 · Completing a reconciliation with no ending balance

Today a reconciliation can be created with its ending balance left empty, for when the statement hasn't arrived yet. When you press c on it, the app now needs that figure before it can complete.

A · Ask for the balance, then the usual complete question — two steps

First a small box asks for the statement's ending balance. enter saves it on the reconciliation, and the header fills in end and diff. Then the same "Complete reconciliation?" question you already know appears, and only y completes.

The complete question itself carries a line for the balance. enter saves the balance and completes in one go. You don't see the difference before completing.

Fewer key presses, but it breaks one habit: every other complete is confirmed with y, and here it is enter. If the batch turns out not to add up, you see it only after it is completed, and then you undo with u.

Part 2 · A new category no longer needs a colour

The New Category form makes colour optional, the way the New Account form already does. Skip it and the engine gives the category its default blue. Editing a category is unchanged: it keeps whatever colour it has.
