Accounts carry their numbers

An account gets its bank and the last 4 digits of its card or account number, and an optional alias. It shows as BCP 5678, or as its alias when it has one, on every screen. Cash and people have no number: their alias is their name, and it is required.

When a card is replaced, the new card is added to the same account from a day on: the name follows the new card, and the old number stays in the account's history. A month's PDF whose number is not the account's number for that month is refused.

This page draws the account form (New and Edit), adding a replacement card, Manage ▸ Accounts, the LOG bar's $, and the refused PDF. The statement code line and every other line stay as you know them. Accounts, numbers and amounts are invented, and so are the engine's words in pane 6. Drawn at 80 columns.

5 · The LOG bar's $ — pick A or B

$ picks an account by its name, as today. With names like BCP 5678, either word finds it.

A · the name shown, either wordrecommended

One rule, as today: $ matches what you see. An account under an alias is found by its alias only; $9902 finds nothing.
