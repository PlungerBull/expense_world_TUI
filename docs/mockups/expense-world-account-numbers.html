Accounts carry their numbers

A bank account holds its bank and the last 4 digits of its card or account number, and the engine names it from them: BCP-5678, or BCP until it has a number. That name is never typed. A person keeps the name you type. Your cash will be one account named Efectivo, made by the engine once it supports it.

Already picked, and drawn here as picked: the bank is chosen from the engine's list; a replaced card is new card and new card from on the Edit form, with older numbers read-only as cards before; Manage ▸ Accounts keeps its columns; a refused PDF stays on the account question. The questions are panes 1, 2, 3 and 5. Accounts, numbers and amounts are invented, and so are the engine's words. Drawn at 80 columns.
