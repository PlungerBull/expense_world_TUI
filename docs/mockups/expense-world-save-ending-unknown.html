A save whose ending is not known

You leave a form while it saves, and the engine answers with a server error, or does not answer at all. The save may still have gone through. Panes 1 and 2 show what the app says on the screen you went back to; pane 3 shows what ctrl+q asks while saves are still being sent.

Every pane is the app at 80×24 over invented names. The engine's words (INTERNAL_ERROR — …) are an example: the app shows whatever the engine sends. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
