Removing a currency · x on the account's row

An account holds one or more currencies. You picked 1 C for Manage ▸ Accounts (one row per account, a PEN and a USD column) and 6 A for adding a currency (c on the account's row lists what it does not hold). This page draws how a currency is removed, as your answer to question 7 of catch-up-currencies-6-main-views.md says: a key of its own on the account's row, x, footer word Remove currency, mirroring c. It lists the account's currencies; the pick asks first, and only y removes it. d on the row still deletes the whole account.

A refused removal reads in the engine's words alone, with no line of the app's added: the account's last currency (LAST_CURRENCY), a currency holding transactions (HAS_TRANSACTIONS, without the "archive it: press a" line, your answer 4 A), a currency in a live statement (IN_LIVE_STATEMENT) and an archived account (ACCOUNT_ARCHIVED). Panes 4 and 5 draw the first two; the other two read the same way. After a removal the list reads fresh from the engine.

This is one design to approve, not options to pick between. Every name and amount is made up: Banco Inventado (PEN, USD), Visa Inventada (PEN, USD, statement code VIN), Caja Chica (PEN), Cuenta Prueba (PEN, nothing in it), the archived Ahorro Viejo (PEN, USD) and the people Ana Ejemplo, Rosa Modelo and Luis Ficticio. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
