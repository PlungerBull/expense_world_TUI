The LOG bar · a long account name in the peek's count

The LOG bar's peek, the list under the bar of the picked account's latest rows, is 72 columns wide at 80. Its last line counts what it shows: 7 of 1716 in <account> · PEN · newest first. With a long account name that line no longer fits.

Every screen is drawn by the app itself at 80×24, with an invented account and invented rows. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
