To answer

1 · The LOG bar's legend at 80 columns: A (two lines, as today), B1, B2 or B3? Recommended: B1 — the legend stays the grammar alone at every width, and keys live in the footer like every other screen's.
2 · A peek title too long for the panel: A or B? Recommended: A — it matches the count line under it, and a name that long is rare.
3 · A ? card taller than the screen: A or B? Recommended: B — on a wide terminal you read every key at once; only a small one makes you scroll.
7 · The "no statement" Edit form's heading: A or B? Recommended: A — a positive amount carries its + wherever it is named.
