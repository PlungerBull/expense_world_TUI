An opening balance stays on its account

An opening balance shows on the ledger as a row in the @Opening category. The engine no longer lets that row move to another account or another category. Its amount, date, title and note can still change. To give an account a different opening balance, or to move one, you use the account's own Edit form (the opening and as of lines you already confirmed).

Two places in the app can try to move such a row: its Edit form on the ledger (⏎), and the c and b keys on selected rows. Screen 1 has a choice and screen 2 has a choice; both options are drawn, with the recommended one marked. The engine's exact words are stand-ins. The rows are invented.

1 · ⏎ on an opening balance — the account and category lines

The ledger's Edit form, opened on the opening balance of Sueldo Soles. Today it offers all seven lines. On an opening balance it no longer offers account and category: either they still show but cannot be changed, or they are left off. Date, title, amount, hashtags and note work as today.

2 · b or c on a selection that includes an opening balance — the other rows go, that one is refused

On the ledger you select rows and press b (move to an account) or c (set the category). The app sends each row on its own, as it does today. The engine refuses the opening balance and accepts the others. The note works as it does today for any refused row: its number, then the engine's words. The choice is whether a line saying what to do follows the engine's words, as the other refusals now get.

3 · The LOG bar — nothing changes

The LOG bar (+) only writes new rows; it never edits a row already on the ledger, and a typed line cannot name @Opening. So it can never try to move an opening balance, and it gets no drawing.
