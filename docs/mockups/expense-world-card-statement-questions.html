A card's statement on one screen · the questions to pick

The engine keeps a card's monthly statement as one thing for the whole card: one name, one set of days, one status, and a starting and ending balance for each of its accounts, one per currency. Its lines come in the paper's order, both currencies in one numbering. Today's screens still show one account at a time. These screens show the card's statement whole, as the bank's paper prints it.

Each question below is drawn with one pane per option, lettered as in the spec catch-up-cards-statements-4-statement-screen.md; the recommended one is tagged. Questions 1 (the screen's name) and 8 (a transaction moved to its card's other account) are about words and are not drawn; the screens keep today's word, Reconciliations.

The cards, lines and amounts are invented: Visa Signature holds Visa Signature PEN and Visa Signature USD, statement code SIG; Interbank Visa holds a PEN and a USD account, no statement code; BCP Ahorros holds one account, Sueldo Soles; BBVA Digital holds one, Cuenta Digital Dólares. Card balances are signed as the paper shows them: negative is what you owe. The button at the top right switches to a light terminal.
