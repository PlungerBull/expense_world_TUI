Split · three moments on the split screen

The split screen is built. Its mockup (expense-world-split-action.html) draws splitting a purchase, splitting it again and a refusal. It does not draw these three moments: title words typed on a part's line, esc with parts typed and not sent, and typing part of a category's name. For each one, the variant marked as built is what the app shows today; the other is the change on offer.

The purchase is FARMACIA, -100.00 on 2026-10-11 from Sueldo Soles, split into -60.00 @salud and -40.00 @hogar #casa. The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · A title typed on a part's line — -40 @hogar lejía

Part 1 (-60.00, SALUD) is in the list. On part 2's line you type -40 @hogar lejía and press ↵. lejía has no // in front, so the grammar reads it as title words, the way the LOG bar reads a line's loose words as its title. A part keeps the purchase's title, FARMACIA.

The LOG bar's own question, with its three answers, worded for parts. The LOG bar reads 2 rows are staged and not written. and [enter] keep editing [d] discard [^s] save first. ⏎ and esc keep you on the screen, d leaves with nothing sent, and ^s sends the split and leaves only if the engine takes it. It asks only when the list holds a part you typed or changed. A line still in the bar is not a part, as in the LOG bar.
