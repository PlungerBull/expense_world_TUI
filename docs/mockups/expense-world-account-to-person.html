Turn an account into a person, and back

On Manage ▸ Accounts you turn a bank account into a person, or a person back into a bank account. Its name, currency, balance and transactions stay as they are. The account only moves from the banks to the people, on Accounts and on Home. Each currency is its own account, so ALEJO PEN and ALEJO USD are turned one at a time.

A person is never offered where only bank accounts belong. Once ALEJO PEN is a person, it no longer appears in a bank file's "Which account is this file?" or on the Reconciliations screen, the same as Eliana today.

Screens 1, 2, 4 and 5 each offer a choice, every option drawn, the recommended one marked. Screen 3 has nothing to choose. Nothing is built until you pick. The accounts and amounts are invented. The engine's refusals are its real words, exactly as the engine sends them, shown whole the way every refusal in the app is.

4 · When the engine refuses — a bank account with an opening balance
