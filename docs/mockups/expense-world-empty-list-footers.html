Empty lists: footers and key cards

When a list has nothing in it, some keys have no row to act on. The ? card already leaves those out on most lists. This page fixes the lists whose card still lists them, and asks whether the footer at the bottom of the screen should leave them out too.

Only empty lists change. With one row or more, every footer and card stays as you know it. Drawn at 80 columns.
