M3 · The ledger (Transactions), in plain words

What happens today. The ledger shows one screenful at a time: "rows 41–60 of 214, page 3 of 11". pgdn swaps in the next screenful. If you make the terminal window bigger while on page 3, the page grows to fit and you stay on the same rows.

What changes. The engine now hands out rows only as "the next batch after this one". It no longer does "jump to row 41". That is fine while the window stays the same size. The question is only what happens when you resize the window in the middle of the ledger:

  • A · Stay where you are. You keep looking at the same rows. But if you then press pgup to go back, the earlier pages come back a different size, so you may see a few rows twice, or miss a few, until you are back at the top.
  • B · Jump back to the top. The ledger returns to the newest rows, the same as opening it again. Filters and selections stay. No doubled or missing rows.
  • C · The ledger works like your M6. No pages at all: it is one list that grows. pgdn adds the next rows underneath, and you scroll with ↑↓. Resizing just shows more or less of the same list, so the question disappears. Drawn below.
