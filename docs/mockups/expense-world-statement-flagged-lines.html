Completing a statement · the lines it flagged

Completing a statement no longer stops when a line dated in it is in no statement: the engine completes it and names those lines flagged for review. A line counts when it is in no statement and dated from the previous statement's last day to this one's last day; on a card, on both currencies.

The engine names them only in its answer to the complete: once you leave the statement, the app cannot list them again (they stay in the ledger, in no statement, and show under u No batch). ⏎ Open opens a line in its Edit form, as it does in the ledger. A completed statement with no flagged line shows nothing new. The example is a card statement; the rows are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.
