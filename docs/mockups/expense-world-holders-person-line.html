People on holders

The engine now puts people on holders, as it does bank accounts: one holder per person, holding at most one account per currency. ALEJO in soles and in dollars can be one person holder holding two accounts.

On Manage ▸ Accounts the Holder column is filled for people too. The question is whether a person's Edit form gets the same holder line as a bank account's, to put two people of one name on one holder. The names and amounts are invented.

1 · Manage ▸ Accounts, people in the Holder column — shared by both options

The Holder column, which you picked for bank accounts, now names a person's holder too, where it showed —. ALEJO in soles sits on the holder ALEJO; his dollar account ALEJO USD sits on a holder of its own, named after it.

Two holders for one man: ALEJO and ALEJO USD. The question is whether you can put them on one.

Option A — a person's Edit form gets the holder line

e on ALEJO USD shows a holder line, as a bank account's form does. Its list holds only person holders with no dollar account, and last, a new holder of its own. Bank holders are never offered: Sueldo Soles and Efectivo hold no dollars, but they are banks.

^s moves the account onto ALEJO in one write. The holder it left held nothing more, so the engine deleted it with the move. The account keeps its name, balance and transactions; both of ALEJO's rows now read ALEJO in the Holder column.

A refusal you should not meet
If the holder picked became a bank holder between opening the list and ^s (someone pressed t on it meanwhile, on another screen or app), the engine refuses the move and nothing is saved. Its words show under the holder line: CONFLICT — Must be a holder of the account's kind: a real account goes on a bank holder, a person on a person holder. Since the list never offers a bank holder, this is drawn here only in words.
Option B — a person's form has no holder line

A person's Edit form stays as today. People share a holder only when made together (see the New person page). Each person keeps the holder it was made on.
