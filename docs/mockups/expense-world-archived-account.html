An archived account is frozen

The engine now freezes an archived account. Of its transactions only the category, the hashtags and the note change; a title that is still empty can be filled once. Their amount, date, title and account stay as they are, and they can't be split or deleted. Its reconciliations don't change at all. Unarchiving the account (Manage ▸ Accounts, a) lifts every one of these.

Where you meet this today: the ledger lists every transaction, an archived account's included, and looks the same on them. The Reconciliations screen lists only active accounts, so an archived account's reconciliations don't appear there. Each screen below has a choice, every option drawn, the recommended one marked. Nothing is built until you pick. The rows are invented; the engine's words are its real ones.
