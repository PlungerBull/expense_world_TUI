Part 6 leftovers · four small picks

These are the open questions of “What part 6 of the currencies catch-up turned up” (docs/specs/catch-up-currencies-6-leftovers.md): four places where the app as built differs from an earlier drawing, or was never drawn. Each pane is today's screen, changed only by the option it shows; the words are the app's own as it renders now.

Every drawing uses made-up accounts: Banco Inventado (PEN and USD), Caja Chica (PEN only) and Visa Inventada (PEN and USD). Today is Saturday 31 October 2026. The engine's sentence in question 4 is drawn as the engine words it.

Please pick one letter per question. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
