A new statement's balances, and the close's notes · two picks

The engine now makes a statement the account's, with a starting and an ending balance for each currency it covers. It needs a starting balance only for a currency that held money when the statement starts, and the app can't tell beforehand which currencies those are: nothing the engine offers today says what a currency held on a past day.

These are questions 1 and 4 of catch-up-currencies-7-statement-screen.md, drawn on the new statement screens. Your earlier answers are kept in every drawing: question 2 B, a currency the statement doesn't cover shows no row; question 3 B, a blank starting balance reads a dim —. Question 5 needs no drawing.

Every drawing uses the same made-up account as the currency picks: VISA Infinite Sapphire, holding soles and dollars, statement code VIS. Its months:

· Julio 2026 – completed; no dollar movement and no dollars owed at the start, so it doesn't cover USD.
· Octubre 2026 – ends with -380.70 owed in soles and -91.10 owed in dollars.
· Noviembre 2026 – the new statement being made, 2026-10-26 → 2026-11-25. Both currencies held money at its start, so the engine needs both starting balances.

Grey refusal lines are the engine's own words; the sentences here are stand-ins, since the contract says only what they name. Pick one letter per question. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.

Question 1 · The New statement form

n on VISA Infinite Sapphire opens one form for the whole account. It sends one statement with an entry for each currency typed. How does it ask for the balances, given the app can't know which currencies need a starting balance?
1 A · a starting and an ending balance for each currency, all optional – n on VISA Infinite Sapphire
Today's form, with one pair of balance lines for each currency of the account, each named by its currency. A currency left empty is not sent. If the engine needs it, it says so by name, and the cursor goes to that currency's starting balance.

The new mark: needed · held -91.10 on 2026-10-25 beside each starting balance, the figure being the engine's. On a month where dollars held 0.00 the day before, the dollar lines read optional with no mark. The form fills in nothing for you. Needs an engine change (a read of what each currency held on a day), so it waits until the engine coders build it. Until then the form works as 1 A, and the engine still has the final say.
