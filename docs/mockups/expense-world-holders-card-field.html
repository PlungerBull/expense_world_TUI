A card on the account's own screens

Your idea: no Cards screen. Manage ▸ Accounts gets a Card column, and a bank account's Edit form gets a card line where you pick the card it sits on. Picking another card is pairing; picking a new card of its own is unpairing. n New makes a card with its accounts in one form (your pick, question 2 C). A person is on no card, so a person's form has no card line.

The example is the Visa: its dollar account Visa Signature USD sits on a card of its own, and goes onto Visa Signature, which holds the soles account. The words in the engine's refusal are its real ones; the names and amounts are invented. Three things are left to pick, at the bottom.

7 · question c · the app's word

The engine calls this a holder: a credit card is one, and so is a savings account of one currency. You have been saying card. The word shows in the Card column, the card line and every note.

A: card, as these screens say. B: holder, the engine's word, shared by every app.

To pick: a · a new card of its own made at once (A, recommended) or through the New card form (B). b · a card's name and code changed on its accounts' Edit forms (recommended), or never after New. c · the word: card (A) or holder (B).
