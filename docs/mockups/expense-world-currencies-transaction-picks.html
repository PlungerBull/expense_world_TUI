A transaction's currency · four picks

An account now holds one or more currencies, and a transaction names its account and its currency as two separate fields. You settled that the currency is picked as its own field, like any other field. This page decides how that looks in four places: the LOG bar (question 8), the Edit form (question 9), the ledger (question 10) and the ledger's moving of selected rows (question 11).

These are questions 8 to 11 of catch-up-currencies-6-main-views.md. Each screen is today's, changed only by the option it shows. Under every option, no list shows "account · code" rows any more: an account is picked alone.

Every drawing uses made-up accounts: Banco Inventado (PEN and USD), Caja Chica (PEN only) and Visa Inventada (PEN and USD). Today is Saturday 31 October 2026. Red refusal lines stand for the engine's own words; the sentences drawn are illustrative.

Pick one letter per question (and 8·i or 8·ii). The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
