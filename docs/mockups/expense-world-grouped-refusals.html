Refused rows · grouped by reason

When the engine refuses several rows of one save, it now names every one of them. These two screens show those rows grouped by the engine's reason, as you asked the engine, or one line per row, as today. A row that is already in the ledger is not a refusal: it counts under already-present (sheet import) or gets its ✓ (LOG bar), and is not listed here. The rows are invented; the reasons are the engine's words.

Pick one letter for each screen. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

  The date falls inside a completed reconciliation's period. rows 512, 513, 514, 515

One line per kind of error. The row numbers are the sheet's lines. A long list of rows wraps onto the next line.

Screen 3 — the engine's words are often short and general, e.g. Must not be null. for a missing date or a missing category
A — the field the engine names, then its words, then the rowsrecommended
expense import sheet Presupuesto.xlsx --apply
Transactions: created 4183, already-present 0, failed 5
Failures (5 rows):
  category_id: Must not be null. rows 12, 40
  date: Must not be null. row 77
  amount_cents: Must not be zero. rows 90, 91

The field is the engine's own name for it (category_id, date), as the other screens show a refusal. Two errors with the same words stay apart and say what they are about. Applies to the LOG bar too.
