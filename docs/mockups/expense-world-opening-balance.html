Set an account's opening balance, from the app

An opening balance is what an account already held on the day you started tracking it. It lives on the account's own Edit form (e on Manage ▸ Accounts) as two more lines after the colour: the amount and its date.

This page is your picks drawn out, for you to confirm before anything is built. Screen 6 is new: you have not seen it before. The accounts and amounts are invented.

6 · The account saves, the opening balance is refused new · not seen before

One save can do two things: save the account's own changes, and set the opening balance. Here the colour was changed to green and an opening balance was typed with a date the engine will not take.

The note says which half went through (the green colour is saved) and which was refused, in the engine's words. The form stays open on the refused line with what you typed, so you fix the date and save again; that second save sends only the opening balance. esc leaves with the account saved and no opening balance. (The engine's words are stand-ins until read from the engine.)
