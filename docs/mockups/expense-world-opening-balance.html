Set an account's opening balance, from the app

Today an account's starting balance can only be set with a typed command (or by the spreadsheet import). These screens put it in the app, on the account's own screen.

Screens 1, 3 and 6 each have a choice to make, with both options drawn and the recommended one marked. Screens 2, 4 and 5 have no choice. Nothing is built until you pick. The accounts and amounts are invented.

1 · Where it lives — its own key, or two more lines on the Edit form

An opening balance is what an account already held on the day you started tracking it. The app has no screen for one account alone, so the account's screen is the Manage ▸ Accounts list, with the cursor on the account.

2 · The form — drawn for 1 A; the amount

The house form: one question at a time, the answers listed under it, ⏎ to move on, ^s to save, esc to leave without saving. The amount is taken exactly as typed. The app never adds or flips a sign: 350.00 is 350.00 you have, -350.00 is 350.00 you owe (a card's starting debt). The account's currency is shown so there is no doubt what the number is in.

4 · When the engine says no — one drawing each, no choice to make

A refusal shows the way every failed save in the app does: a note in the bottom-right corner whose first lines are the engine's own answer. Nothing is saved, and the form stays open with what you typed so you can correct it. (The engine's exact words are stand-ins in these drawings until they are read from the engine.)

A person's balance is a debt, built only from the rows you record, so the engine refuses to give one a starting figure. o opens the form on any row and the engine answers on save; the app does not decide for it.

5 · After it is set — one drawing, no choice to make

The form closes and the Accounts list is read again from the engine: first page, cursor on the first row, and the account's balance now includes the opening figure (Efectivo, 0.00 → 350.00). The opening balance is kept as one row in the ledger under the built-in category @Opening: it counts in the account's balance and is left out of the monthly income and spending on Home, because it is not money that moved. No column is added to this list.

6 · If the account already has one — the engine keeps one per account

An account has at most one opening balance. Pressing o on an account that already has one can go two ways.

o always opens the empty form of screen 2. Saving a second one is refused in the engine's words, with one line of the app's own saying where the existing one is: a row in the ledger you change or delete like any other. It works with the engine as it is today, but you find out only after filling the form in.
