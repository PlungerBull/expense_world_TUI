Every screen in the app

One page with every screen expense has today, grouped by kind, and how each one leads to the next. Use it to follow a whole flow, and to see which screens a change touches before you approve it.

This is a reference picture, not a choice: nothing here is waiting on a pick. Each screen is drawn as it works on 2026-10-16; the rows are invented. The last group lists the screens that are drawn and waiting on your pick, and which of the screens above each one would change.

How to read a screen: the top line is where you are (Manage ▸ Accounts), the bottom line is the keys that work there. Blue keys lead somewhere; esc always goes one step back.

1 · Before the app

What happens before Home: the first launch, and the one thing you type in the terminal instead of opening the app.

Setup — the first launch only

When there are no settings yet, or they cannot be read, the app opens here instead of Home. It asks for the engine's address and your key, asks the engine whether they work, and saves nothing until they do. If the engine knows the key but has no profile for it, it also asks your name (the time zone is read from this Mac).

3 · Capture

How money gets into the ledger. + on Home or on the ledger opens the LOG bar: you type lines, they stack up, and ctrl+s writes them all. Dropping a bank file onto the bar starts the import, three screens per file.

LOG bar — one typed line per transaction

Type a line like Almuerzo $sueldo @comida #almuerzo -18.50 ayer and ↵ stages it; a line the app cannot read is refused in place with the reason. Staged rows wait above until ctrl+s writes them. As soon as the line names an account, that account's latest rows show underneath.

A reconciliation — one statement, ticked row by row

The statement's figures on top (begin, end, and the diff that should reach 0.00), then the rows: on a draft, the ones in it and the ones in its period that are in none; space puts a row in or out. c completes it (asking for the ending balance first if it is empty), u reverts a completed one to draft, d deletes the reconciliation itself — its transactions stay.

New / Edit account — a bank account or a person

New asks type (bank or person), name, currency and an optional colour. Edit asks the same, with currency fixed, and a bank account adds its opening balance: the amount and the day the account held it. Emptying the amount of an existing one deletes it, after asking.

6 · Coming — drawn, waiting on your pick

Screens drawn for open work and not built. Each page has lettered variants and a "To answer" box; nothing is built until you pick. Here is which of today's screens each one changes, so you see the knock-on before you choose.

  • The bank upload — the import's preview and done screens learn about bank lines and the days whose totals disagree.
  • Split parts in the ledger — Transactions and the filtered ledger show a purchase's parts under it, folded or open.
  • The split screen — a new sub screen off the ledger: one row split into parts with their own category and hashtags.
  • Editing a part — Edit a transaction says which fields change every part of the purchase.
  • The pending hashtag on Home — Home's unfolded categories name untagged rows #Pendiente, and ⏎ on them works.
  • The pending hashtag on Manage — Hashtags marks the built-in one, and its delete question changes.
  • Expense World XP · a sketch, needs a spec from you — a strip on Home, toasts when something is earned, a monthly recap and a new Character screen.
