The cash account

Your cash becomes one account named Efectivo: no bank, no card number, no statements. The engine names it, and nobody has one until they ask for it, so the app offers to make it once, while you have none (an archived one counts). It can be archived, never deleted.

Drawn as the screens will look once "Accounts carry their numbers" is built, since that spec reshapes the account form first. Accounts and amounts are invented. Drawn at 80 columns.

1 · Where you make it — pick A or B

Making it asks for the same things as any account: which currencies it holds and, optionally, each currency's opening balance (the cash in your wallet that day). One save makes it, or nothing.

3 · The cash account on Manage ▸ Accounts — no pick: what the engine allows

The cash account can't be renamed, given a bank, number or statement code, turned into a person, or deleted. So on its row t and d leave the footer (archive it instead), and its Edit form holds only what can change.

Cash never appears where only a bank account belongs: the Statements screen, a PDF upload's "Which account is this statement?" and a bank download's account question. Everywhere else (the LOG bar's $, the ledger, moves, the account pickers) it works like any account.

To pick: one letter per pane with a question, e.g. 1A 2A. Pane 3 is drawn for you to object to, not to pick.
