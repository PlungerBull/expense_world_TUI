The pending hashtag · Home, the Edit form, the LOG bar

The engine now has a built-in pending hashtag, #Pendiente, the hashtag twin of @Pendiente: every transaction with no hashtag carries it, and it comes off by itself when a real hashtag goes on. This page is where you meet it day to day: Home's hashtag rows, the ledger they open, and tagging a row on the Edit form and the LOG bar. Its own row on Manage ▸ Hashtags is in its own mockup.

The rows are invented. Use the button at the top right to see the page on a light terminal.

1 · Home's hashtag rows — COMIDA unfolded with →

Every transaction now carries a hashtag: one you have not tagged sits under the built-in #Pendiente, as one with no category sits under @Pendiente. So under a category, the row that read (no hashtags) is now the pending hashtag's own row, #Pendiente: a built-in name that never changes. @Pendiente now unfolds too: its rows wait for a hashtag as well. The cursor is on COMIDA's pending row.
