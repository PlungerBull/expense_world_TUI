a archives the whole card

A bank account is archived with its card: a on either of the Visa's accounts archives both, Visa Signature PEN and Visa Signature USD, and a again unarchives both. There is no way to archive one of them alone. A savings account is a card of one, so for it nothing changes. A person is on no card, and its a stays as it is.

The question is how the list and its note show that the other account went too. Drawn with the Card column of question 1 A. The accounts and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.
