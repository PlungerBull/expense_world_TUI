Editing a split transaction

Your design, drawn: the parts stay rows in the ledger, and ⏎ on any part opens the same Edit form as any transaction. On top, what every part shares; below, every part with its own amount, category, hashtags and note, and the line adding them up. Parts are added and dropped in the form. A transaction never split opens the same form, with one part. The reconciliation screens keep showing a split transaction as one line.

The parts of the design you settled are drawn without options. One question is left: what you can do on a line in the “no batch” list. The screens that stay as they are today are drawn from the app's own code, so they look exactly as they do now.

The transaction is invented: Tottus Jockey Plaza -100.00 on Sueldo Soles, 2026-10-11, operation 47391026, split into -60.00 COMIDA (#casa, “verduras”) and -40.00 HOGAR (“lejía y papel”). Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

2 · ⏎ on a part · the Edit form, with its parts — Ledger ▸ ⏎ on Tottus Jockey Plaza

The same Edit form as today: one bar on top, a hint under it, and a summary below. The summary now has two parts. On top are the lines every part shares: date, title and account, the three the ledger's Edit form shows today. Below is a table with one line per part: its amount, category, hashtags and note. Under the table is the line adding the parts up, as the Split screen (s in the ledger) shows it today. The table shows every part, so it doesn't matter which part you pressed ⏎ on.

Moving around: ⏎ and ⇥ go down the form one line at a time, from DATE to TITLE, ACCOUNT, then part 1's four fields, then part 2's, as today. The bar names the part: PART 1 AMOUNT. The part you are on is marked ▌ 1 in the table. ⏎ on the last part's NOTE saves, as ⏎ on NOTE does today. Adding and dropping: ^n adds an empty part at the end and puts the bar on its AMOUNT. ^x drops the part the bar is on. Letters go into the bar, so these two keys are control keys: ^x is the key that drops a part on the Split screen today, and n is “New” across the app. ^x shows only on a part's line, and only while there are two parts or more.

4 · A reconciliation's list — already so today: one line for a split transaction, and only ticking

Already so today, nothing changes. A reconciliation lists the bank's lines in the bank's columns only (date, title, operation, amount), so Tottus Jockey Plaza is one line with its total, -100.00, and nothing on it says it is split. On this screen you only tick: space puts the line under the cursor in or out of this reconciliation, every part with it. ⏎ does nothing on a line. c, u and d complete, revert or delete the whole reconciliation, never a line. Once completed, the list is read-only: read-only while completed · u revert to edit · r refresh · esc back. To change a line, you go to the ledger, or to “no batch” while it is in no reconciliation (question 1 below).

What the engine has to answer first — for the app's builders, not a question for you

This page shows what you see. Behind it, a few points have to be checked with the engine, and some may become requests to the engine coders before this can be built. One save, one write: today, the parts and the title are sent in one write, and the date, account and operation in another. A save that changes both would need two writes, and if the second were refused, only half would be saved. The parts keep who they are: today, changing a split's parts gives every part a new identity, so changing one part's category would replace every part. Locked before you type: screen 3 needs the engine to say, when the app reads the transaction, that it sits in a completed reconciliation. Down to one part: today a split needs two parts or more, so dropping to one takes several writes. The first, second and fourth are already asked of the engine, in to-engine/one-write-edits-a-whole-bank-line.md. The third is not asked yet.

To answer: one letter, e.g. 1 A.
  • Question 1 · a line in “no batch”: A (as today: ⏎ opens the new Edit form, d deletes after the question) or B (only looking; changes and deletes from the ledger). Recommended: 1 A.
  • Sections 1 to 4 draw the design you settled, so they ask nothing. If a key (^n, ^x), a hint or a line reads wrong to you, say the words you want instead.
