d deletes the whole account

Until the main views are redrawn (spec 6), Manage ▸ Accounts shows one row per account and currency, each named <account> · <code>. But d deletes the whole account, every currency of it at once, and the engine deletes only an account with nothing booked in any currency. The question is what d asks. The accounts and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.
