Where a card lives in the app

In the engine a card is a thing of its own: one bank product holding its accounts, one per currency. The Visa is a card of two accounts, Visa Signature PEN and Visa Signature USD; each savings account is a card of one; a person is on none. The statement code belongs to the card, and archiving archives the whole card.

The question is where you see and change a card: its name, its code and which accounts are on it. The accounts, names and amounts are invented. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

1 · The word — what the app calls the engine's holder

A savings account is a holder of one account; the Visa is a holder of two. The engine keeps card for the bank's product, a credit card. Every screen on these pages says card until you pick.
