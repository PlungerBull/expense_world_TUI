Putting a statement in order

Each line of a statement gets its place: its number on the paper statement, top to bottom, both currencies of a card mixed. The engine keeps the statement's lines in that order and builds each line's statement line from it, which no screen shows. A statement on an account with a statement code can't be completed until it is in order. This screen is where you put it in order, once per statement, by reading the paper next to it.

Picked by you on 2026-10-24, from the redraw: the screen opens with the lines already in date order, so you move only the few lines the paper prints elsewhere, with shift+↑↓; a paper line the ledger doesn't have comes in from the bank's file, through the import door, never typed here; a line added after the order was saved waits at the end of the order, marked new. Opening in date order needs the engine ask “The statement order lists a statement's unplaced lines oldest first”; the screens are drawn as if it has landed.

The card is the one from the card-statement mockup: Signature PEN and Signature USD, code SIG, statement Octubre 2026, 2026-09-26 → 2026-10-25. Lines and amounts are invented; the engine's refusals are its real words. Use the button at the top right for a light terminal.

1 · Opening the screen — o on Octubre 2026 · nothing to choose here

The first time you open a statement, every line of both halves is already in the order, by date, oldest first, soles and dollars mixed, numbered 001, 002 … from the top. That is the engine's suggestion (the engine ask “The statement order lists a statement's unplaced lines oldest first”), not yet saved: the line under the list says so. Your paper is mostly by date, so you read it next to the screen and fix only the lines that sit elsewhere on the paper. When the date order is already right, ctrl+s saves it as it is.

3 · A line on the paper the ledger doesn't have — from the bank's file, as you picked · nothing to choose

Such a line comes in by itself from the bank's file, through the import door the app already has: drop the card's BCP file on the LOG bar, and every line of the file the ledger lacks is created under @Pendiente, both currencies (expense-world-import-door.html). Done before you open the order, the new lines are simply among the others, by date. This screen creates nothing and has no empty slots. One step is still by hand today: ticking the new lines into the statement on its screen (space); doing that by itself too is listed for the next piece of work.
