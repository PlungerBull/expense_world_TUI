A save that answers after you leave its screen

You press esc while a save is still on its way to the engine. Today, when the engine answers, the app says nothing. From now on the message the screen would have shown comes over the screen you went to.

Panes 0 and 1 show what changes. Panes 2 to 6 each need a pick, A or B. Everything is drawn at 80 columns, with invented accounts, rows and amounts. The screens behind the messages are sketches; only the messages are proposed. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.

What to pick

  • Pane 2 · A or B: the first line of a refused save you left. Recommended B, which names the save, since you may have left more than one.
  • Pane 3 · A or B: how a bulk edit you left names its refused rows. Recommended B, since the row numbers point at a list you are no longer on.
  • Pane 4 · A or B: after you leave, does one refused opening balance stop the ones after it? Recommended B, so only what the engine refused is left to redo.
  • Pane 5 · A or B: a completed statement's flagged lines, after you left it. Recommended B, so no screen opens by itself.
  • Pane 6 · A or B: balances saved, completion never asked. Recommended B, so you never think a statement is completed when it is not.

Saves where esc already waits for the engine are unchanged: the LOG bar, a bank file's import and a statement's PDF close.
