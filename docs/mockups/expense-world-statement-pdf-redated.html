The statement preview when a line moves one day

When you drop a statement PDF, the preview lists every row of the paper and says which are already in your ledger. Sometimes your line for a purchase is one day off the bank's date: you wrote it the day you paid, the bank printed the next day.

The engine now pairs such a line anyway, when it is the same direction and amount one day before or after the paper's row, and moves your line to the bank's day when the statement is closed. The question is how the preview shows that a line will move. Everything else on the preview stays as it is today. The names, days and amounts are invented.

Today · the preview with a one-day-off line — what goes away

The paper prints RAPPI PERU, 100.00 out, on 2026-03-15; your line for it is on 2026-03-14. Today the engine does not pair them: the row reads New (closing would create a second line) and a warning under it points at yours. The dollar row 6 is the same, the other way: the paper on 03-05, your line on 03-06.
