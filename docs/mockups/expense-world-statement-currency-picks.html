A statement's currencies · three picks

The engine now makes a statement the account's, with a starting and an ending balance for each currency it covers. Two things are new: a statement may not cover one of the account's currencies (a month with no dollar line and no dollars owed at the start), and a statement may hold a currency whose starting balance is blank (when a dollar line is ticked into a month that had no dollars, the month gains dollar balances, both blank).

These are questions 2 and 3 of catch-up-currencies-7-statement-screen.md. Your answer is used twice: now, on today's screens, by part 2 of the catch-up (drawn here), and later, on the new statement screen part 7 draws.

Every drawing uses one made-up account: VISA Infinite Sapphire, holding soles and dollars, statement code VIS. Its months:

· Julio 2026 – no dollar movement and no dollars owed at the start, so it doesn't cover USD.
· Agosto and Septiembre 2026 – both currencies, both add up.
· Octubre 2026 – a draft. A dollar line was ticked in after it was made, so its USD starting balance is blank.

Grey refusal lines are the engine's own words, quoted from the contract. Pick one letter per question. The button at the top right switches between a light and a dark terminal.
